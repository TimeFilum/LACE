# Third-party code and model dependencies

The runner, data loading infrastructure, forecasting baselines, layers, and
utilities contain code adapted from the THUML Time-Series-Library project:

<https://github.com/thuml/Time-Series-Library>

The upstream MIT notice is included in
`third_party/Time-Series-Library-LICENSE.txt`.

Pretrained models are downloaded separately. Their upstream projects are:

- Moirai: <https://github.com/SalesforceAIResearch/uni2ts>
- Chronos: <https://github.com/amazon-science/chronos-forecasting>
- TimesFM: <https://github.com/google-research/timesfm>
- Transformers: <https://github.com/huggingface/transformers>

The original LACE contributions are licensed under the MIT License in the root
`LICENSE` file. The upstream THUML copyright and MIT notice remain preserved
separately. External packages, pretrained models, and datasets retain their own
licenses and terms.
